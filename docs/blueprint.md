# **App Name**: DTC Insights Navigator

## Core Features:

- Landing Page Sections: Landing page with sections: Why DTC Brands Choose Us, Our Research Solutions for DTC Brands, Our Pricing Philosophy, What Our Clients Say, Brands We Worked With, About Us, and Contact.
- Contact Form: Contact form to capture lead information (name, email, company).
- Responsive Design: Responsive design for viewing on various devices.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated Gold (#D4AF37) for sophistication and trustworthiness, reflecting a high standard.
- Background color: Desaturated Carbon (#36454F) creates depth, emphasizes the gold and conveys sophistication.
- Accent color: Dark Blue (#003049), used sparingly, contrasts sharply with the gold to draw attention to key details.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern sans-serif font to highlight a geometric, fashionable and elegant brand identity
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanistic sans-serif for modern easy readability
- Simple line icons to represent the different sections and solutions.
- Clean and spacious layout to ensure readability and engagement.
